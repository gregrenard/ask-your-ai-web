# Messages — Ask your AI about me

Single source of truth for the words on the site. Mission: help the web become conversational. Name: Ask your AI about me (the .me is the domain and each owner's own name). Vocabulary: fact sheet (never "brief"), box, check (the free diagnosis), kit (the $10 setup).

## Hero

- Title: Make your website conversational.
- Sub: Paste your address. Your visitors' own AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) learns your facts, answers from them, and sends the right people to you.
- Field and button: your website address / Check my site.
- Under the field: Free. No account. Not a chatbot: your visitors bring their own AI.

## Why we exist (the manifesto)

- The web was built to be read. Now it is being asked.
- More and more of your visitors talk to an AI before they talk to you. It answers from whatever it finds. You never see the answer.
- Most websites don't know how to answer back. They were made for browsers, not for conversations.
- Our mission: help the web become conversational. Starting with yours.

## How (three pillars)

- Be understood: your facts, checked against your own site, on one page AI can read.
- Be conversational: one small box; your visitor's own AI opens already briefed and ends in your contact form.
- Stay in control: you approve every word; nothing is sent without your visitor.

## What

- Conversation title: An AI that knows what it doesn't know. Caption: Real test on ChatGPT. Names removed, the visitor is fictional.
- Never: it never invents a fact, it never speaks for you, it never sends a word without your visitor.
- Price: $10. Once. Your fact sheet, the box and the install steps for your platform. You host it. It's yours.
- Planned: Kept current. Every month we re-read your site and show you what changed. You approve each change. Price not set.
- Closing: Let your visitors' AI talk to your website. Button: Check my site.

## Rules

- No invented numbers, customers or testimonials. "More and more" instead of a statistic nobody measured.
- No personal names, no named example sites. The example is a fictional Example Studio.
- Never name competitors.
- No early line breaks: paragraphs and titles wrap only when they run out of room.
- The 5-second test: show the hero for five seconds to five non-technical people and ask what it does. If three say "a chatbot", switch the title to "Let your visitors' AI talk to your website."
