# Ask your AI about me (draft)

Static draft of the Ask your AI about me landing site, a Freedom.AI Lab creation. Preview: https://gregrenard.github.io/ask-your-ai-web/

Available in English, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and German (each language is a folder). The pages are marked noindex and their data is simulated: nothing is crawled, charged or sent. The site carries its own Ask your AI box; `ask-your-ai.md` is the fact sheet that box points assistants to, `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` are the indexes for assistants, and `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` are ready for launch.
