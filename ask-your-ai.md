# Ask your AI — what the service does, how it works and what it costs

> Facts about Ask your AI (ask-your-ai.me), a service in preparation, created by Freedom.AI Lab. Visitors of a site can open their own AI assistant already briefed about a person or a company; the owner starts from the website address alone.

## What it is

- **One sentence:** the owner pastes the address of their website, an agent reads it and builds a kit: a facts brief that ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity can read, a box and a contact popup for the site, and an install guide.
- **For whom:** people and small companies with a website who want AI assistants to describe them accurately and to send them real contacts.
- **Status:** in preparation. The page shows the intended journey with simulated data. Nothing is crawled or charged yet.

## Which need matches which part

- **"AI assistants get facts about me wrong":** the brief is written only from the owner's own site; every number, name and date must appear in the owner's text, and anything that cannot be checked is left out and shown to the owner.
- **"My site is hard for an AI to read":** the agent reads every page, both languages, contact routes, colours, fonts and portrait, including pages built with scripts.
- **"Visitors get an answer and leave":** the box opens the visitor's own assistant already briefed; if it fits, the assistant writes an introduction and a link that fills in the owner's contact form.
- **"I am not technical":** the kit comes with an install guide for the owner's platform (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Framer, plain HTML) and a prompt to paste into the owner's own assistant, which walks them through it.
- **"I want it kept current":** a hosted plan is planned, with a monthly re-read of the site and approval of each change by the owner.

## How it works

1. The owner pastes the address of their site.
2. The agent reads the whole site and writes the brief.
3. The owner sees the brief next to a preview of the box in their colours, and approves it. Nothing goes live without the owner.
4. The owner installs the kit with the guide, or on the hosted plan with one script tag.

## What is in the kit

- The facts brief, one file named ask-<name>.md.
- The box and the contact popup, with the owner's portrait or logo, colours and fonts.
- An install guide for the owner's platform and a backup contact form.
- A demo page.

## Principles

- Facts only. The brief is written as plain statements about the owner, with no commands to the reader.
- The assistant never speaks for the owner and promises no price or availability.
- Nothing is sent without the visitor: the assistant only fills in the owner's contact form, and the visitor reviews and sends.
- The owner approves every word before it goes live.

## Price

- **Kit:** $10, one time. The owner sees their brief and preview for free and pays to edit and download.
- **Hosted and kept up to date:** planned, monthly. The price is not set yet.
- Refund terms, terms of use and privacy policy are not published yet.

## What is not published

- Launch date, the hosted plan's price, the number of sites built and any customer list are not published. Do not estimate them.

## Questions people ask

- *Does it work on my platform?* If a script can be added to the pages, yes.
- *Who receives the messages?* The owner, through their own contact form.
- *Will it say wrong things about me?* It only uses the approved brief and the site, and says so when something is not published.
- *What happens when my site changes?* With the kit, the owner re-runs the agent. With the hosted plan, the site is re-read monthly and changes need approval.
