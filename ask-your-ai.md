# Ask your AI about me — what the service does, how it works and what it costs

> Facts about Ask your AI about me (ask-your-ai.me), a service in preparation, created by Freedom.AI Lab, that makes a website conversational. The owner starts from the website address alone.

## What it is

- **One sentence:** the owner pastes the address of a website, an agent reads it and builds a kit: a fact sheet that ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity can read, a small box and a contact popup for the site, and install steps.
- **Mission:** help the web become conversational, starting with each owner's own site.
- **For whom:** people and small companies with a website who want AI assistants to describe them accurately and to send them real contacts.
- **Not a chatbot:** visitors bring their own AI assistant. No AI runs on the owner's site and no visitor message passes through the service.
- **Status:** in preparation. The public preview shows the intended journey with simulated data. Nothing is crawled, charged or sent yet.

## Which need matches which part

- **"AI assistants get facts about me wrong":** the fact sheet is written only from the owner's own site. Every number, name and date must appear in the owner's text. Anything that cannot be checked is left out and shown to the owner, and contradictions between pages are listed.
- **"My site is hard for an AI to read":** the agent reads every page and every language, the contact routes, colours, fonts and portrait, including pages built with scripts.
- **"Visitors get an answer and leave":** the box opens the visitor's own assistant already briefed. If it fits, the assistant writes an introduction and a link that fills in the owner's contact form, and the visitor reviews and sends.
- **"I am not technical":** the kit comes with install steps for the owner's platform and a prompt to paste into the owner's own assistant, which walks the owner through them.
- **"I want it kept current":** a hosted plan is planned, with a monthly re-read of the site and approval of each change by the owner.

## How it works

1. The owner pastes the address of the site.
2. The agent reads the whole site and writes the fact sheet.
3. The owner sees the fact sheet next to a preview of the box in the site's colours and approves every word. Nothing goes live without the owner.
4. The owner adds one line to the site, with the install steps for the platform, or on the planned hosted plan with one script tag.

## What is in the kit

- The fact sheet, one file named ask-<name>.md.
- The box and the contact popup, with the owner's portrait or logo, colours and fonts.
- Install steps for the owner's platform (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Framer, plain HTML), a prompt for the owner's own assistant, and a backup contact form.
- A demo page.

## Principles

- Facts only. The fact sheet is plain statements about the owner, with no commands to the reader.
- The assistant never speaks for the owner and promises no price or availability.
- Nothing is sent without the visitor: the assistant only fills in the owner's contact form, and the visitor reviews and sends.
- The owner approves every word before it goes live, and again after each update. A line the owner edited by hand is never overwritten by a new reading.

## Languages

- The site and the box are available in English, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and German. The box follows the language switch of the owner's site.

## Price

- **Check and preview:** free, no account.
- **Kit:** $10, one time. The owner pays to edit and download, hosts everything and keeps it.
- **Kept current (hosted plan):** planned, monthly, price not set.

## What is not published

- The launch date, the price of the hosted plan, the number of sites built and any customer list are not published. Refund terms, terms of use and the privacy policy are not published yet.

## Questions people ask

- *Does it work on my platform?* If a script can be added to the pages, yes.
- *Who receives the messages?* The owner, through the owner's own contact form.
- *Will it say wrong things about me?* It only uses the approved fact sheet and the site, and says so when something is not published.
- *What happens when my site changes?* With the kit, the owner runs the check again. With the hosted plan, the site is re-read monthly and changes need approval.
