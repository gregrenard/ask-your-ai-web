# How Ask your AI about me works

Ask your AI about me turns the address of a website into three things: a fact sheet that AI assistants can read, a small box for the site, and install steps. This page describes the three steps an owner takes and what a visitor then sees.

## The three steps for the owner

1. **Paste your address.** An agent reads the whole site: every page, every language, the contact routes, the colours, the fonts and the portrait or logo. No account and no files are needed for the check.
2. **Check your fact sheet.** Every fact on the sheet must come from the owner's own pages. A fact that cannot be verified is left out and shown to the owner, and a contradiction between two pages is listed instead of being resolved silently. The owner sees the sheet next to a preview of the box in the site's colours and approves every word before anything goes live.
3. **Add one line.** The owner pastes a script tag into the site. The kit includes install steps for WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Framer and plain HTML, and a prompt to paste into the owner's own assistant (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini), which walks the owner through the steps.

## What a visitor sees

- A small box in the corner of the site. One click opens the visitor's own AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, or Google's AI mode for Gemini) already briefed with a link to the owner's fact sheet.
- The assistant answers from the fact sheet and says so when something is not published.
- If the visitor wants to get in touch, the assistant writes a short introduction and a link that fills in the owner's contact form. The visitor reviews the message and sends it. Nothing is sent without the visitor.
- The box and the popup follow the site's own language switch.

## What it is not

- It is not a chatbot. No AI runs on the owner's site, and no visitor message passes through Ask your AI about me.
- It never invents a fact, never speaks for the owner, and never promises a price or an availability on the owner's behalf.

## Status
