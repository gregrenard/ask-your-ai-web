Questions

Will the AI say wrong things about me?

It only uses the fact sheet you approve. Every fact is checked against your own pages, and when something isn't published the AI says so.

Does it work on my platform?

If you can add a script to your pages, yes: WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Framer or plain HTML.

Who receives the messages?

You do, through your own contact form. The AI only fills it in. The visitor reviews and sends.

What happens when my site changes?

With the kit, you run the check again. With the hosted plan, we re-read your site every month and you approve what changed.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You paste an address, read one page, and add one line.
