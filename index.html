Questions

Will the AI say wrong things about me?

It can only draw on the brief you approve and on your site. The agent checks every fact against your text, flags contradictions between your pages, and you read the result before anything is published. If something is not published, the assistant says so instead of guessing.

Does it work on my platform?

If you can add a script to your pages, yes. The install guide is written for WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, Framer and plain HTML, and the paste-in prompt lets your own assistant walk you through it.

Who receives the messages?

You do, through your own contact form. The assistant prepares the message and a link that fills the form. The visitor reviews and sends it. We never see your messages in the kit version.

What happens when my site changes?

With the kit, you re-run the agent whenever you like. With the hosted plan, we re-read your site every month, show you what changed, and you approve it.

Do I need to be technical?

No. You paste an address, read a page, and follow numbered steps with your own assistant at your side.
